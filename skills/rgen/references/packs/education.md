# Data pack: education (Education 1.0.0)

Generated by rgen-cli/site-packs.sh from the pack; edit lookup_data/packs/education/skill.md in cuiqGEN instead.
Read only when `rgen packs list` shows this pack as licensed.

Use for higher-education data: student records (SIS), programs and majors, course enrollments
and grades, attendance, retention and graduation, and LMS clickstreams. Programs are real CIP 2020
codes weighted by national IPEDS completions at each level (associate, bachelor's, master's,
research doctorate), and students' sex follows each program's share of awards to women. A student
number fixes the student (level, program, demographics, ability, cohort, status, GPA, credits), so
student lists, enrollments and LMS events agree without joins. Grades follow ability and course
difficulty; withdrawal is likelier for weaker, first-generation and associate students. All
students and records are synthetic.

## Chains (put each driver in its own column, then pass the column)

| Driver column | Dependent providers |
|---|---|
| `student` (row number, `edu_student(n)`, or `edu_enrolled_student(n)`) | `edu_student_id(student)`, `edu_level(student)`, `edu_cip(student)`, `edu_program(student)`, `edu_field(student)`, `edu_sex(student)`, `edu_entry_age(student)`, `edu_residency(student)`, `edu_first_gen(student)`, `edu_pell(student)`, `edu_hs_gpa(student)`, `edu_cohort_year(student)`, `edu_entry_term(student)`, `edu_status(student)`, `edu_exit_date(student)`, `edu_class_year(student)`, `edu_credits(student)`, `edu_gpa(student)` |
| student | `edu_course(student)` (STRUCT: code, title, diff) |
| course code | `edu_section(course_code)` → `edu_instruction_mode(section)`; `edu_credit_hours(course_code)` |
| student + course diff | `edu_grade(student, course_diff)` → `edu_grade_points(grade)`, `edu_absences(student, grade)` |
| `edu_lms_event` | `edu_lms_time(event, term_start)`, `edu_late_submission(student, event)` |

Standalone: `edu_lms_device`. Status, cohort, class year and credits take `as_of_year`
(default 2025: fall 2025).

## Pitfalls

- Use the same student range everywhere (students 1..N, `edu_enrolled_student(N)` for records).
- Use `edu_enrolled_student` for current-term records: `edu_student` also returns graduated and
  withdrawn students.
- Read STRUCT fields with `struct_extract(course, 'code')`, not `course.code`.
- `edu_course` draws a new course on each call: keep it in one column and extract code, title and
  diff from it.
- Programs are U.S. CIP codes; professional doctorates (MD, JD) are not included.

## All providers

Use in `-p` without the `rand_` prefix; in TOML `derived` expressions with it.

- `edu_absences(s,grade)`: class meetings missed in a term (of about 30): few for strong students, many behind D, F and W grades — dependent: edu_absences(student, grade)
- `edu_cip(s)`: CIP 2020 code of the major (52.0201), weighted by national completions at the level (IPEDS) — dependent: edu_cip(student)
- `edu_class_year(s,as_of_year)`: class standing of enrolled students (freshman to senior, first or second year, year_N for graduate students); NULL otherwise — dependent: edu_class_year(student, as_of_year)
- `edu_cohort_year(s,as_of_year)`: fall the student entered (up to 6 years before as_of_year, default 2025) — dependent: edu_cohort_year(student, as_of_year)
- `edu_course(s,as_of_year)`: course the student takes this term (STRUCT code, title, diff): general education early in an undergraduate degree, then courses of the major's subject at the student's level (BIOL 201 Cell Biology, CS 501 Advanced Algorithms) — dependent: edu_course(student, as_of_year)
- `edu_credit_hours(course_code)`: credit hours: 3 for most courses, 4 for many lab sciences — dependent: edu_credit_hours(course_code)
- `edu_credits(s,as_of_year)`: credits earned: the full program (60, 120, 36 or 72) once graduated, progress so far otherwise — dependent: edu_credits(student, as_of_year)
- `edu_enrolled_student(n,as_of_year)`: random student number (1 to n) enrolled in fall of as_of_year, for enrollments and LMS events — dependent: edu_enrolled_student(n, as_of_year)
- `edu_entry_age(s)`: age at entry: 18-19 for most bachelor's students, older and spread out for associate and graduate students — dependent: edu_entry_age(student)
- `edu_entry_term(s,as_of_year)`: entry term (Fall 2022) — dependent: edu_entry_term(student, as_of_year)
- `edu_exit_date(s,as_of_year)`: graduation date (mid-May) or withdrawal date; NULL while enrolled — dependent: edu_exit_date(student, as_of_year)
- `edu_field(s)`: broad field (CIP family): Business, Health Professions, Engineering, ... — dependent: edu_field(student)
- `edu_first_gen(s)`: first-generation college student (neither parent has a bachelor's degree) — dependent: edu_first_gen(student)
- `edu_gpa(s)`: cumulative GPA (0-4): higher for stronger students, lower in hard programs, about 0.4 higher for graduate students — dependent: edu_gpa(student)
- `edu_grade(s,course_diff)`: letter grade (A to F, W for withdrawal) from the student's ability, the course's difficulty and the day: about a third As, harder STEM courses grade lower — dependent: edu_grade(student, course_difficulty)
- `edu_grade_points(grade)`: quality points of a letter grade (A 4.0 ... F 0.0); NULL for W — dependent: edu_grade_points(grade)
- `edu_hs_gpa(s)`: high-school GPA of undergraduates (NULL for graduate students), tracking the student's ability — dependent: edu_hs_gpa(student)
- `edu_instruction_mode(section)`: in_person, evening or online, from the section number — dependent: edu_instruction_mode(section)
- `edu_late_submission(s,event)`: whether a submission came after the deadline (about 12 percent, fewer for strong students); NULL for other events — dependent: edu_late_submission(student, event)
- `edu_level(s)`: degree level sought: associate, bachelor, master or doctoral — dependent: edu_level(student)
- `edu_lms_device()`: device used: laptop, desktop, phone or tablet
- `edu_lms_event()`: learning-platform event: course_view, content_view, video_play, assignment_view/submit, quiz_start/submit, forum_view/post, grade_view, file_download
- `edu_lms_time(event,term_start)`: event timestamp in a 16-week term from term_start (a Monday): busier before midterms and finals, submissions bunched on Sunday nights before the deadline — dependent: edu_lms_time(event, term_start)
- `edu_pell(s)`: Pell Grant recipient (undergraduates; more likely for first-generation students) — dependent: edu_pell(student)
- `edu_program(s)`: program title from CIP 2020 (Registered Nursing/Registered Nurse) — dependent: edu_program(student)
- `edu_residency(s)`: in_state, out_of_state or international (international students are mostly graduate students) — dependent: edu_residency(student)
- `edu_section(course_code)`: section number (W01 online, 501 evening) — dependent: edu_section(course_code)
- `edu_sex(s)`: F, M or X, following the program's share of awards to women (nursing mostly F, engineering mostly M) — dependent: edu_sex(student)
- `edu_status(s,as_of_year)`: status in fall of as_of_year: enrolled, graduated or withdrawn (dropout likelier for lower ability, first-generation and associate students, mostly in the first two years) — dependent: edu_status(student, as_of_year)
- `edu_student(n)`: random student number from 1 to n (default 10000); every other provider derives the student from it
- `edu_student_id(s)`: student ID (S + 8 digits), unique per student number — dependent: edu_student_id(student)

## Templates

Print one with `rgen templates <pack>/<name> > file.toml`, edit, then `rgen run --config file.toml out.duckdb`.

- `education/enrollments`: Fall 2025 course enrollments of currently enrolled students: course code and title, section, mode, credit hours, letter grade, grade points, absences
- `education/lms-events`: Learning-platform clickstream for fall 2025: student, course, event, timestamp (deadline peaks), device, late flag
- `education/programs`: Program catalog: CIP 2020 code and title, field, subject code, award level, national completions (IPEDS 2024) and share of awards to women
- `education/students`: Student records: ID, level, CIP program and field, sex, entry age and term, residency, first-generation, Pell, high-school GPA, status, exit date, class year, credits, GPA
