# Data pack: insurance (Insurance 1.0.0)

Generated by rgen-cli/site-packs.sh from the pack; edit lookup_data/packs/insurance/skill.md in cuiqGEN instead.
Read only when `rgen packs list` shows this pack as licensed.

Use for insurance books of business: personal auto, homeowners, renters and term life policies,
and their claims. Premiums follow rating factors (driver age, vehicle value, coverage, deductible,
state, prior claims; home value, age, roof, state; issue age, sex, tobacco class, term). VINs carry
the make's real manufacturer code, the model-year code and a valid check digit. Claim causes follow
the line, the auto coverage and the state's dominant peril (hurricane, hail, wildfire, winter);
amounts, report and close dates, status, paid and reserves are consistent. All values are synthetic
and illustrative, not filed rates.

## Pitfalls

- Claim frequency comes from row counts: rgen picks foreign keys uniformly, so keep one policy
  table per line and size its claims table by frequency (roughly auto 9%, home 5.5%, renters 2%,
  life well under 1% of policies per year), as the templates do.
- Copy the coverage, deductible, state and dates from the policy with match lookups (foreign key
  named `policy_ref`, not `policy_id`), and keep the loss date inside the policy term.
- `ins_close_date` and statuses are relative to `as_of` (default 2026-09-30); pass the same
  `as_of` to `ins_report_date` and `ins_close_date` if you change it, and keep loss dates before it.
- Liability causes ignore the deductible; liability-only auto policies have a NULL deductible and
  only liability causes.
- VINs are valid but random after the manufacturer code: NHTSA decoders confirm the check digit
  and manufacturer, not a specific trim. They are not real vehicles.

## All providers

Use in `-p` without the `rand_` prefix; in TOML `derived` expressions with it.

- `ins_adjuster()`: claims adjuster ID from a team of 60
- `ins_annual_miles(vuse)`: annual miles driven for a vehicle use — dependent: ins_annual_miles(use)
- `ins_auto_coverage()`: auto coverage: full (with collision and comprehensive) or liability only
- `ins_auto_premium(driver_age,coverage,vehicle_value,deductible,st,prior_claims,term_months)`: auto premium (USD per term) from driver age, coverage, vehicle value, deductible, state and prior claims — dependent: ins_auto_premium(driver_age, coverage, vehicle_value, deductible, state, prior_claims, term_months)
- `ins_body(vid)`: body style: Sedan, SUV, Pickup, Minivan, Coupe or Wagon — dependent: ins_body(vehicle)
- `ins_claim_number(line,report_date)`: claim number with line prefix and report year — dependent: ins_claim_number(line, report_date)
- `ins_claim_status(lc,close_date,loss,deductible)`: claim status: open or in_litigation until closed, then closed_paid or closed_no_payment (always when the loss is under the deductible) — dependent: ins_claim_status(cause, close_date, loss, deductible)
- `ins_close_date(report_date,lc,as_of)`: date the claim closed, or NULL while still open on the as_of date (default 2026-09-30) — dependent: ins_close_date(report_date, cause, as_of)
- `ins_construction()`: home construction type
- `ins_deductible(line,coverage)`: deductible (USD) for a line; NULL for liability-only auto and for life — dependent: ins_deductible(line, coverage)
- `ins_driver_age()`: age of the rated driver, 16 to 89
- `ins_dwelling_coverage(home_value)`: dwelling coverage A (rebuild cost, below market value) — dependent: ins_dwelling_coverage(home_value)
- `ins_face_amount()`: life face amount (USD)
- `ins_home_form()`: homeowners policy form: HO-3, HO-5 or HO-8
- `ins_home_premium(dwelling,deductible,st,year_built,roof_age,prior_claims)`: annual homeowners premium (USD) from dwelling coverage, deductible, state, age of home, roof and prior claims — dependent: ins_home_premium(dwelling, deductible, state, year_built, roof_age, prior_claims)
- `ins_home_value(st)`: market value (USD) of a home, typical for the state — dependent: ins_home_value(state)
- `ins_insured_age()`: issue age of a life insured, 18 to 77, mostly 25 to 50
- `ins_liability_limits()`: auto liability limits in thousands (bodily injury per person / per accident / property damage)
- `ins_life_premium(age,sex,risk_class,face_amount,term_years)`: annual term life premium (USD) from age, sex, risk class, face amount and term — dependent: ins_life_premium(age, sex, risk_class, face_amount, term_years)
- `ins_line()`: line of business: auto, home, renters or life
- `ins_loss_amount(lc,face_amount)`: ground-up loss (USD) typical of a cause (glass hundreds, fires and wildfires tens to hundreds of thousands); the face amount for life — dependent: ins_loss_amount(cause, face_amount)
- `ins_loss_cause(line,coverage,st)`: loss cause for a line: liability-only auto has only liability causes, home causes follow the state's peril (hurricane, hail, wildfire, winter) — dependent: ins_loss_cause(line, coverage, state)
- `ins_make(vid)`: vehicle make — dependent: ins_make(vehicle)
- `ins_model(vid)`: vehicle model — dependent: ins_model(vehicle)
- `ins_model_year(vid)`: model year the model was sold in, leaning recent — dependent: ins_model_year(vehicle)
- `ins_paid(lc,loss,deductible,status)`: amount paid to date (USD): loss net of the deductible when paid, partial payments on some open claims, 0 otherwise — dependent: ins_paid(cause, loss, deductible, status)
- `ins_peril(st)`: dominant property peril of a US state: hurricane, hail, wildfire, winter or general — dependent: ins_peril(state)
- `ins_policy_number(line)`: policy number with a line prefix (PA, HO, RT, TL) and 10 digits ending in a Luhn check digit — dependent: ins_policy_number(line)
- `ins_policy_status()`: policy status: active, cancelled, lapsed or non_renewed
- `ins_prior_claims()`: claims in the last 3 years
- `ins_renters_premium(property_limit,deductible,st)`: annual renters premium (USD) — dependent: ins_renters_premium(property_limit, deductible, state)
- `ins_renters_property_limit()`: renters personal property limit (USD)
- `ins_report_date(loss_date,lc,as_of)`: date the claim was reported: days after the loss, longer for injuries and liability, never after as_of — dependent: ins_report_date(loss_date, cause, as_of)
- `ins_reserve(lc,loss,deductible,status,paid)`: outstanding case reserve (USD) on open claims, higher in litigation; 0 once closed — dependent: ins_reserve(cause, loss, deductible, status, paid)
- `ins_risk_class(tobacco)`: life underwriting class; tobacco classes for smokers — dependent: ins_risk_class(tobacco)
- `ins_roof_age(year_built)`: roof age in years, never older than the home — dependent: ins_roof_age(year_built)
- `ins_siu_referral(lc,loss_date,report_date)`: referral to the special investigations unit: rare, likelier for theft, fire, injuries and late reports — dependent: ins_siu_referral(cause, loss_date, report_date)
- `ins_subrogation(lc)`: whether the insurer pursues recovery from a third party (common for collisions) — dependent: ins_subrogation(cause)
- `ins_term_months(line)`: policy term in months: 6 or 12 for auto, 12 otherwise — dependent: ins_term_months(line)
- `ins_term_years(age)`: term length (10, 20 or 30 years) available at an issue age — dependent: ins_term_years(age)
- `ins_vehicle()`: vehicle id (make and model) weighted by US popularity; drives the other vehicle providers
- `ins_vehicle_use()`: vehicle use: commute, pleasure, business or rideshare
- `ins_vehicle_value(vid,model_year)`: current market value (USD) of a vehicle and model year, depreciated from its price — dependent: ins_vehicle_value(vehicle, model_year)
- `ins_vin(vid,model_year)`: 17-character VIN with the make's real manufacturer code (WMI), the model-year code and a valid check digit — dependent: ins_vin(vehicle, model_year)
- `ins_year_built()`: year a home was built, following the US housing stock

## Templates

Print one with `rgen templates <pack>/<name> > file.toml`, edit, then `rgen run --config file.toml out.duckdb`.

- `insurance/auto`: Personal auto policies (driver, vehicle with VIN, coverage, limits, deductible, premium) and their claims with causes, amounts and lifecycle
- `insurance/home`: Homeowners policies (home value, dwelling coverage, construction, roof, deductible, premium by state) and claims whose causes follow the state's peril
- `insurance/life`: Term life policies (issue age, sex, tobacco, risk class, face amount, term, premium) and death claims paying the face amount
- `insurance/vehicles`: Vehicle list for fleet or telematics tests: VIN with valid check digit, make, model, model year, body and value
