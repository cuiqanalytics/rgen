# Data pack: telecom (Telecom 1.0.0)

Generated by rgen-cli/site-packs.sh from the pack; edit lookup_data/packs/telecom/skill.md in cuiqGEN instead.
Read only when `rgen packs list` shows this pack as licensed.

Use for mobile telecom data: subscribers, SIMs and handsets, call and SMS detail records (CDRs),
data sessions, monthly usage and bills, and churn. A subscriber number fixes everything about a
subscriber (country, real operator and MCC/MNC, mobile number, IMSI, ICCID, IMEI, handset, plan,
activation, churn), so subscriber lists, CDRs, sessions and monthly usage agree without joins.
MSISDNs are valid mobile numbers of the country (libphonenumber), IMEI and ICCID carry Luhn check
digits, and IMSIs start with the operator's MCC/MNC. Churn follows the plan (prepaid churns most)
and complaints. All subscribers and records are synthetic; amounts are illustrative USD.

Standalone: `telecom_direction` (MO/MT). Dates default to `as_of = 2026-09-30`.

## Pitfalls

- Use the same subscriber range everywhere: subscribers numbered 1..N, and
  `telecom_active_subscriber(N, ...)` or `telecom_subscriber(N)` for records.
- Use `telecom_active_subscriber` (not `telecom_subscriber`) for records in a time window, or
  `telecom_event_time` returns NULL for subscribers who churned before it.
- Never nest: `telecom_msisdn(telecom_subscriber())` draws a new subscriber per call; keep `sub` as
  a column.
- Churned subscribers keep their numbers in the data; filter on `telecom_status(sub)` for the
  active base.
- TACs (IMEI digits 1-8) are synthetic per handset model; IMEIs validate but do not identify real
  devices.

## All providers

Use in `-p` without the `rand_` prefix; in TOML `derived` expressions with it.

- `telecom_activation_date(sub,as_of)`: activation date, spread over the 6 years before as_of (default 2026-09-30) — dependent: telecom_activation_date(sub, as_of)
- `telecom_active_in_month(sub,month_start,as_of)`: whether the subscriber was active in the month (activated before it ended, not churned before it began) — dependent: telecom_active_in_month(sub, month_start, as_of)
- `telecom_active_subscriber(n,from_date,to_date,as_of)`: random subscriber (1 to n) that was active at some point between from_date and to_date, for CDRs and sessions in a window — dependent: telecom_active_subscriber(n, from_date, to_date, as_of)
- `telecom_apn(sub)`: access point name: internet, corp.internet for business, iot.m2m for IoT — dependent: telecom_apn(sub)
- `telecom_app_category(sub)`: app category of a data session: video, social, browsing, messaging, music, gaming, maps, software_update, cloud_sync; telemetry for IoT — dependent: telecom_app_category(sub)
- `telecom_bill_amount(sub,overage,other)`: monthly bill in USD: plan fee plus overage and other charges, plus the country's taxes and fees (12 percent US, VAT in Europe, 30 percent Brazil) — dependent: telecom_bill_amount(sub, overage, other)
- `telecom_cause_code(oc)`: ITU-T Q.850 release cause: 16 normal clearing, 17 busy, 19 no answer, 41 temporary failure for drops, 1/21/34/38/41 for failures — dependent: telecom_cause_code(outcome)
- `telecom_cell_id(sub)`: serving cell as MCC-MNC-eNodeB-sector; 70 percent of records come from the subscriber's three usual sites (home, work, commute) — dependent: telecom_cell_id(sub)
- `telecom_charge(sub,rtype,dest,secs,roaming)`: rated charge in USD outside the plan: international and roaming minutes (per started minute) and messages; domestic usage is included (0) — dependent: telecom_charge(sub, record_type, destination_type, duration_sec, roaming)
- `telecom_churn_date(sub,as_of)`: date the subscriber left, NULL while active on as_of: monthly hazard about 3.5 percent prepaid, 0.9 percent postpaid, lower for family, business and IoT plans, higher with complaints — dependent: telecom_churn_date(sub, as_of)
- `telecom_churn_reason(sub,as_of)`: why a churned subscriber left (inactivity is the top prepaid reason; network quality and customer service after complaints); NULL while active — dependent: telecom_churn_reason(sub, as_of)
- `telecom_complaints(sub)`: customer-care complaints logged (0-3); each one raises the churn hazard by 60 percent — dependent: telecom_complaints(sub)
- `telecom_contract_months(sub)`: contract length in months (0 for prepaid) — dependent: telecom_contract_months(sub)
- `telecom_country(sub)`: country of the subscriber (ISO 3166 alpha-2; GB for the United Kingdom), 14 countries weighted by market size — dependent: telecom_country(sub)
- `telecom_data_allowance_gb(sub)`: monthly data allowance in GB, NULL for unlimited plans — dependent: telecom_data_allowance_gb(sub)
- `telecom_destination_type(sub)`: other party: on_net (same operator), off_net, international or voicemail (more international for business) — dependent: telecom_destination_type(sub)
- `telecom_device(sub)`: handset model (budget models on prepaid, flagships on postpaid and business; a Cat-M1 module on IoT plans) — dependent: telecom_device(sub)
- `telecom_device_5g(sub)`: whether the handset supports 5G — dependent: telecom_device_5g(sub)
- `telecom_device_manufacturer(sub)`: handset manufacturer — dependent: telecom_device_manufacturer(sub)
- `telecom_device_os(sub)`: handset OS: iOS, Android or RTOS (IoT) — dependent: telecom_device_os(sub)
- `telecom_direction()`: mobile originated (MO) or terminated (MT)
- `telecom_downlink_bytes(app,secs,rat)`: bytes downloaded: the app's bitrate times the session length (HD video on 5G), or link speed for updates and sync — dependent: telecom_downlink_bytes(app, session_sec, rat)
- `telecom_duration_sec(sub,rtype,oc)`: billable seconds: long-tailed (median about 75 s, longer for business and video), 0 for unanswered calls and messages — dependent: telecom_duration_sec(sub, record_type, outcome)
- `telecom_event_time(sub,from_date,to_date,as_of)`: timestamp between from_date and to_date while the subscriber is active (NULL if never): consumers peak at lunch and evening, business in office hours, IoT around the clock — dependent: telecom_event_time(sub, from_date, to_date, as_of)
- `telecom_iccid(sub)`: 20-digit SIM ICCID: 89 (telecom), the country calling code, the operator's MNC as issuer, and a Luhn check digit — dependent: telecom_iccid(sub)
- `telecom_imei(sub)`: 15-digit IMEI with a Luhn check digit; one synthetic TAC per handset model, so models group by the first 8 digits — dependent: telecom_imei(sub)
- `telecom_imsi(sub)`: 15-digit IMSI: the operator's MCC and MNC plus the subscriber's MSIN — dependent: telecom_imsi(sub)
- `telecom_mcc(sub)`: mobile country code of the home operator (E.212) — dependent: telecom_mcc(sub)
- `telecom_mnc(sub)`: mobile network code of the home operator (2 or 3 digits) — dependent: telecom_mnc(sub)
- `telecom_monthly_data_gb(sub)`: data used in a month (GB): around half the allowance on capped plans (prepaid stops at the cap, postpaid can go over), 20-30 GB on unlimited, megabytes for IoT — dependent: telecom_monthly_data_gb(sub)
- `telecom_monthly_fee(sub)`: monthly plan fee in USD, scaled to the country's price level (before tax) — dependent: telecom_monthly_fee(sub)
- `telecom_monthly_sms(sub)`: SMS sent in a month (IoT devices report by SMS) — dependent: telecom_monthly_sms(sub)
- `telecom_monthly_voice_min(sub)`: voice minutes in a month (more for business; 0 for IoT) — dependent: telecom_monthly_voice_min(sub)
- `telecom_msisdn(sub)`: mobile number in E.164 (+447712345678) that libphonenumber accepts as a mobile number of the country — dependent: telecom_msisdn(sub)
- `telecom_operator(sub)`: home mobile operator of the subscriber, weighted by market share in the country — dependent: telecom_operator(sub)
- `telecom_other_party(sub,dest)`: the other party's E.164 number: same country for on_net and off_net, another country for international, a voicemail access number — dependent: telecom_other_party(sub, destination_type)
- `telecom_outcome(rtype,rat)`: call outcome: answered, no_answer, busy, failed or dropped (drops most on UMTS, least on 5G); delivered or failed for SMS and MMS — dependent: telecom_outcome(record_type, rat)
- `telecom_overage(sub,gb)`: data overage in USD: 10 per started GB over the allowance (scaled to the country) on capped postpaid plans; prepaid is throttled instead — dependent: telecom_overage(sub, data_gb)
- `telecom_payment_status(sub)`: bill payment: paid, paid_late or unpaid (worse after complaints); prepaid for prepaid plans — dependent: telecom_payment_status(sub)
- `telecom_plan(sub)`: plan code (PRE-10, POST-UNL, FAM-4, BIZ-STD, IOT-1, ...) — dependent: telecom_plan(sub)
- `telecom_plan_name(sub)`: plan name — dependent: telecom_plan_name(sub)
- `telecom_plan_type(sub)`: prepaid, postpaid or iot — dependent: telecom_plan_type(sub)
- `telecom_ported_in(sub)`: whether the number was ported in from another operator (about 18 percent) — dependent: telecom_ported_in(sub)
- `telecom_rat(sub)`: radio access technology: 5G NR only on 5G handsets and plans (more where 5G coverage is wider), LTE, a little UMTS, LTE-M for IoT — dependent: telecom_rat(sub)
- `telecom_record_type(sub)`: CDR record type: voice, sms, mms or video (SMS only for IoT) — dependent: telecom_record_type(sub)
- `telecom_roaming(sub)`: whether the record was made abroad (2.5 percent consumer, 8 percent business) — dependent: telecom_roaming(sub)
- `telecom_segment(sub)`: customer segment: consumer, business or iot — dependent: telecom_segment(sub)
- `telecom_session_sec(app)`: session length in seconds typical of the app (minutes of video and music, seconds for messaging and IoT telemetry) — dependent: telecom_session_sec(app)
- `telecom_status(sub,as_of)`: status on as_of: active, suspended (non-payment) or churned — dependent: telecom_status(sub, as_of)
- `telecom_subscriber(n)`: random subscriber number from 1 to n (default 10000); every other provider derives the subscriber from it
- `telecom_tenure_months(sub,as_of)`: whole months from activation to churn, or to as_of while active — dependent: telecom_tenure_months(sub, as_of)
- `telecom_uplink_bytes(app,down)`: bytes uploaded relative to the download: tiny for streaming, larger for messaging and gaming, more than the download for cloud backups and IoT telemetry — dependent: telecom_uplink_bytes(app, downlink_bytes)

## Templates

Print one with `rgen templates <pack>/<name> > file.toml`, edit, then `rgen run --config file.toml out.duckdb`.

- `telecom/cdr`: Call and SMS detail records for September 2026 from subscribers active then: type, direction, numbers, destination, RAT, cell, outcome, Q.850 cause, duration, roaming, charge
- `telecom/data-sessions`: Mobile data sessions for September 2026: subscriber, APN, RAT, cell, app category, duration, downlink and uplink bytes
- `telecom/monthly-usage`: One row per subscriber and month (Oct 2025 - Sep 2026): active flag, data, minutes, SMS, overage, bill with taxes, payment status
- `telecom/subscribers`: Subscriber base: country, operator, MCC/MNC, MSISDN, IMSI, ICCID, IMEI, handset, plan, fee, activation, status, churn date and reason, complaints
