# Data pack: energy (Energy 1.0.0)

Generated by rgen-cli/site-packs.sh from the pack; edit lookup_data/packs/energy/skill.md in cuiqGEN instead.
Read only when `rgen packs list` shows this pack as licensed.

Use for electricity data: smart-meter premises, hourly or 15-minute interval reads, rooftop and
utility solar, monthly usage and bills, and grid outages. A meter number fixes the premise (city,
class, home size, AC, heating, EV, rooftop solar, tariff), and a meter plus a timestamp fixes every
read, so interval reads, monthly totals and bills agree and re-runs give the same values. Loads
follow the time of day, weekdays, and the city's temperature (cooling above 22 C, electric heating
below 15 C); solar follows the sun's elevation for the latitude and season and a daily cloud draw
(southern-hemisphere cities have their summer in January). All values are synthetic and
illustrative; amounts in USD.

## Chains (put each driver in its own column, then pass the column)

| Driver column | Dependent providers |

## Recipes

```bash
rgen run -n 1000 -p "energy_meter(500) as meter;energy_city(meter) as city;energy_customer_class(meter) as class;energy_solar_kw(meter) as solar_kw;energy_tariff(meter) as tariff" meters.csv
```

```toml
[[tables]]
name = "reads"
rows = 2400

  [tables.columns]
  row_no  = { derived = "row_number() OVER ()" }
  meter   = { derived = "(row_no - 1) % 10 + 1" }
  ts      = { derived = "TIMESTAMP '2025-07-01 00:00:00' + to_minutes((15 * ((row_no - 1) // 10))::INT)" }
  reading = { derived = "rand_energy_reading(meter, ts, 15)" }
  kwh     = { derived = "struct_extract(reading, 'kwh')" }
  solar   = { derived = "struct_extract(reading, 'solar_kwh')" }

[output]
format = "duckdb"
```

## Pitfalls

- Read STRUCT fields with `struct_extract(col, 'field')`, not `col.field` (rgen would read that
  as a table name).
- Several values of the same interval: use `energy_reading` once and extract fields; the
  single-value providers each recompute the hour.
- `energy_monthly_usage` sums every hour of the month (about 10 ms per meter-month): keep
  monthly grids to a few thousand rows.
- Grids (meter x hour, meter x month) are built from `row_number()`; keep the modulus, the
  division and `rows` in sync, as the template comments explain.
- Temperatures are per city and day, the same for every meter in the city; this pack does not
  read the `weather` pack.

## All providers

Use in `-p` without the `rand_` prefix; in TOML `derived` expressions with it.

- `energy_bill_amount(m,usage)`: monthly bill in USD on the meter's tariff from energy_monthly_usage: fixed charge plus energy (flat, tiered over 600 kWh, peak and off-peak, or a demand charge on max kW), less export credits — dependent: energy_bill_amount(meter, usage)
- `energy_city(m)`: city of the premise: 23 cities from hot deserts to cold continental climates, both hemispheres — dependent: energy_city(meter)
- `energy_country(m)`: country of the premise (ISO 3166 alpha-2) — dependent: energy_country(meter)
- `energy_customer_class(m)`: residential (85 percent), commercial or industrial — dependent: energy_customer_class(meter)
- `energy_customers_affected(cause)`: customers interrupted: thousands in storms, a street for planned work — dependent: energy_customers_affected(cause)
- `energy_export_kwh(m,ts,minutes)`: solar energy sent to the grid in the interval (solar minus consumption, at least 0) — dependent: energy_export_kwh(meter, ts, minutes)
- `energy_feeder()`: distribution feeder code (FDR-C117)
- `energy_has_ac(m)`: whether the premise has air conditioning: almost all homes in hot cities, few in cool coastal ones — dependent: energy_has_ac(meter)
- `energy_has_ev(m)`: whether the household charges an electric vehicle at home (about 9 percent of homes; 7.2 kW evening sessions) — dependent: energy_has_ev(meter)
- `energy_heating(m)`: heating: gas, heat_pump, electric_resistance or oil (gas dominates in cold cities, heat pumps in warm ones) — dependent: energy_heating(meter)
- `energy_home_sqft(m)`: home floor area in square feet (residential only, NULL otherwise); scales the load — dependent: energy_home_sqft(meter)
- `energy_import_kwh(m,ts,minutes)`: energy drawn from the grid in the interval (consumption minus solar, at least 0) — dependent: energy_import_kwh(meter, ts, minutes)
- `energy_kwh(m,ts,minutes)`: electricity consumed in the interval starting at ts (kWh; minutes default 60, use 15 for quarter-hours): load shape by class and weekday, cooling above 22 C, electric heating below 15 C, EV charging — dependent: energy_kwh(meter, ts, minutes)
- `energy_latitude(m)`: latitude of the premise's city — dependent: energy_latitude(meter)
- `energy_meter(n)`: random meter number from 1 to n (default 10000); every other provider derives the premise from it
- `energy_meter_id(m)`: meter serial (MTR + 8 digits), unique per meter number — dependent: energy_meter_id(meter)
- `energy_monthly_usage(m,month_start)`: STRUCT of a month's totals from the same hourly values as energy_kwh: kwh, solar_kwh, import_kwh, export_kwh, peak_kwh (weekdays 16-21), max_kw, avg_temp_c; read fields with struct_extract — dependent: energy_monthly_usage(meter, month_start)
- `energy_outage_cause()`: outage cause: weather, equipment_failure, vegetation, animal, vehicle_accident, planned or unknown
- `energy_outage_minutes(cause)`: outage duration in minutes: long-tailed for storms, short for animals and unknown causes, about 4 hours for planned work — dependent: energy_outage_minutes(cause)
- `energy_outage_start(cause,yr)`: outage start in a year (default 2025): weather outages peak in summer afternoons (storms), planned work on weekday mornings — dependent: energy_outage_start(cause, year)
- `energy_plant_capacity_mw(p)`: AC capacity of a solar plant in MW (5-200, most under 50) — dependent: energy_plant_capacity_mw(plant)
- `energy_plant_city(p)`: city nearest a utility solar plant number — dependent: energy_plant_city(plant)
- `energy_plant_mwh(p,ts)`: plant output in the hour starting at ts (MWh): sun elevation, cloud, tracking gain, and occasional days with half the inverters down — dependent: energy_plant_mwh(plant, ts)
- `energy_plant_tracking(p)`: mounting: single_axis trackers (more output morning and evening) or fixed_tilt — dependent: energy_plant_tracking(plant)
- `energy_read_quality(m,ts)`: read quality flag: actual, estimated (0.4 percent) or missing (0.1 percent) — dependent: energy_read_quality(meter, ts)
- `energy_reading(m,ts,minutes)`: STRUCT with every value of one interval (temp_c, kwh, solar_kwh, import_kwh, export_kwh), computed once: faster than calling the single-value providers side by side; read fields with struct_extract — dependent: energy_reading(meter, ts, minutes)
- `energy_solar_kw(m)`: rooftop solar capacity in kW (0 when none): more installs in sunny cities; 30-250 kW on some commercial roofs — dependent: energy_solar_kw(meter)
- `energy_solar_kwh(m,ts,minutes)`: rooftop solar generation in the interval (kWh): follows the sun's elevation for the latitude, season and hour, cut on cloudy days, 0 at night or without panels — dependent: energy_solar_kwh(meter, ts, minutes)
- `energy_tariff(m)`: rate plan: flat, tiered or tou for homes, ev_tou for EV owners, demand for commercial and industrial — dependent: energy_tariff(meter)
- `energy_temperature_c(m,ts)`: outdoor temperature (C) at the premise: the city's season, a persistent daily anomaly and the daily cycle (same for every meter in the city) — dependent: energy_temperature_c(meter, ts)
