# Data pack: logistics (Logistics 1.0.0)

Generated by rgen-cli/site-packs.sh from the pack; edit lookup_data/packs/logistics/skill.md in cuiqGEN instead.
Read only when `rgen packs list` shows this pack as licensed.

Use for shipping and supply-chain data: ocean container bookings, air cargo, US parcels with
tracking histories, and warehouse movements. Identifiers carry valid check digits (ISO 6346
containers, IATA air waybills, UPS 1Z and USPS tracking numbers, GS1 SSCC). Ports are the 66
largest container ports (UN/LOCODE) and airports are real (OurAirports), so distances, transit
times and rates follow real lanes. Container serials, waybills and tracking numbers are random,
not real shipments.

Templates: `logistics/ocean-containers`, `logistics/air-cargo`, `logistics/parcels` (with JSON
tracking history), `logistics/warehouse-moves`. To get one row per tracking scan, unnest the
`events` column in SQL:
`SELECT tracking_no, e.ts, e.status, e.location FROM parcels, unnest(from_json(events, '[{"ts":"TIMESTAMP","status":"VARCHAR","location":"VARCHAR"}]')) t(e)`.

## Pitfalls

- Never nest: `logistics_destination_port(logistics_port())` draws a different origin than the
  one you keep; generate the origin as its own column.
- Parcel zones and costs are US domestic; states are two-letter codes. Shipments to or from
  Alaska and Hawaii are always zone 8.
- `logistics_delivered_at` is NULL for lost and damaged parcels; keep the outcome column so
  analyses can tell late from lost.
- Rates are illustrative list rates (USD), not market quotes; ocean headhaul from Asia costs
  more than the backhaul.

## All providers

Use in `-p` without the `rand_` prefix; in TOML `derived` expressions with it.

- `logistics_air_distance_km(a1,a2)`: great-circle distance in km between two airports — dependent: logistics_air_distance_km(origin, destination)
- `logistics_air_rate_per_kg(distance_km)`: air freight rate (USD per chargeable kg) for a distance — dependent: logistics_air_rate_per_kg(distance_km)
- `logistics_airline()`: 3-digit IATA cargo prefix of a cargo airline (157 Qatar, 023 FedEx, 176 Emirates, ...)
- `logistics_airline_name(prefix)`: airline name for a cargo prefix — dependent: logistics_airline_name(prefix)
- `logistics_airport()`: IATA code of an airport with scheduled service, cargo hubs (HKG, MEM, ANC, ...) much likelier
- `logistics_airport_city(ap)`: city an airport serves — dependent: logistics_airport_city(iata)
- `logistics_airport_country(ap)`: country of an airport — dependent: logistics_airport_country(iata)
- `logistics_airport_icao(ap)`: ICAO code of an airport — dependent: logistics_airport_icao(iata)
- `logistics_airport_name(ap)`: airport name — dependent: logistics_airport_name(iata)
- `logistics_arrival_delay_days()`: days a vessel arrives after its ETA (about half on time, a long tail of delays)
- `logistics_awb(prefix)`: air waybill number: airline prefix, 7-digit serial and mod-7 check digit (e.g. 157-12345675) — dependent: logistics_awb(airline)
- `logistics_bin_location()`: warehouse bin location zone-aisle-bay-level (e.g. C-14-07-B)
- `logistics_bl_number(carrier_name)`: bill of lading number starting with the carrier's SCAC — dependent: logistics_bl_number(carrier)
- `logistics_carrier(cn)`: ocean carrier moving a container: its owner, or a carrier that leased it — dependent: logistics_carrier(container_number)
- `logistics_chargeable_weight(actual_kg,length_cm,width_cm,height_cm)`: chargeable weight in kg: the greater of actual and volumetric (cm³ / 6000), rounded up to 0.5 kg steps — dependent: logistics_chargeable_weight(kg, length, width, height)
- `logistics_container_description(ctype)`: description of a size-type code — dependent: logistics_container_description(type)
- `logistics_container_number()`: ISO 6346 container number: a carrier or lessor owner code, 6-digit serial and valid check digit
- `logistics_container_operator(cn)`: ocean carrier that owns a container (or leased for lessor boxes) — dependent: logistics_container_operator(container_number)
- `logistics_container_type()`: ISO 6346 size-type code (45G1 40ft high cube, 22G1 20ft dry, 45R1 reefer, ...) in a realistic mix
- `logistics_delivered_at(ship_ts,transit_days,outcome)`: delivery timestamp from ship time, transit days and outcome (later for late and exceptions, NULL when lost or damaged, weekends skipped) — dependent: logistics_delivered_at(ship_ts, transit_days, outcome)
- `logistics_delivery_outcome()`: delivery outcome: on_time, late, an exception (address, damage, weather) or lost
- `logistics_destination_airport(origin)`: destination airport for an origin: another continent 70% of the time, never the origin — dependent: logistics_destination_airport(origin)
- `logistics_destination_port(origin)`: port of discharge for an origin port: another trade region 85% of the time, never the origin — dependent: logistics_destination_port(origin)
- `logistics_flight_number(prefix)`: flight number with the airline's IATA code — dependent: logistics_flight_number(airline)
- `logistics_incoterm()`: Incoterms 2020 rule in a realistic mix
- `logistics_movement_qty(mtype)`: units moved for a movement type: case multiples for receipts, a few units per pick, signed for adjustments — dependent: logistics_movement_qty(type)
- `logistics_movement_type()`: warehouse movement type, picks most common
- `logistics_ocean_rate(pol,pod,ctype)`: all-in ocean freight per container (USD): distance-based, dearer from Asia (headhaul) than back, reefers dearer — dependent: logistics_ocean_rate(pol, pod, type)
- `logistics_ocean_transit_days(distance_nm)`: scheduled port-to-port transit in days for a distance at 16 knots plus port calls — dependent: logistics_ocean_transit_days(distance_nm)
- `logistics_parcel_carrier()`: US parcel carrier: USPS or UPS
- `logistics_parcel_cost(service,zone,weight_lb)`: shipping cost (USD) for a service, zone and weight — dependent: logistics_parcel_cost(service, zone, weight_lb)
- `logistics_parcel_service(carrier)`: service level of a parcel carrier (Ground, 2nd Day Air, Priority Mail, ...) — dependent: logistics_parcel_service(carrier)
- `logistics_parcel_weight_lb()`: parcel weight in pounds (median about 2, long tail to 150)
- `logistics_port()`: UN/LOCODE of a major container port, weighted by annual TEU
- `logistics_port_country(pc)`: country of a port — dependent: logistics_port_country(locode)
- `logistics_port_name(pc)`: port name — dependent: logistics_port_name(locode)
- `logistics_port_region(pc)`: trade region of a port (East Asia, Northern Europe, North America West, ...) — dependent: logistics_port_region(locode)
- `logistics_sea_distance_nm(pol,pod)`: sailing distance in nautical miles between two ports (great circle times a detour factor for the trade lane: Suez, Panama, Cape) — dependent: logistics_sea_distance_nm(pol, pod)
- `logistics_sscc()`: 18-digit SSCC pallet or case label with a recurring company prefix and valid GS1 check digit
- `logistics_teu(ctype)`: twenty-foot equivalent units of a container type — dependent: logistics_teu(type)
- `logistics_tracking_events(ship_ts,delivered_at,outcome,zone)`: tracking history as a JSON array of {ts, status, location}: label created, pickup, hub scans by zone, exceptions, out for delivery, delivered — dependent: logistics_tracking_events(ship_ts, delivered_at, outcome, zone)
- `logistics_tracking_number(service)`: tracking number in the carrier's format with a valid check digit: UPS 1Z (recurring shipper numbers), USPS 22-digit IMpb, else 10-digit mod 7 — dependent: logistics_tracking_number(service)
- `logistics_transit_days(service,zone)`: scheduled transit in business days for a service and zone — dependent: logistics_transit_days(service, zone)
- `logistics_voyage()`: voyage number with direction suffix (e.g. 045E)
- `logistics_zone(from_state,to_state)`: US shipping zone 1-8 from the distance between two states (8 to and from Alaska and Hawaii) — dependent: logistics_zone(from_state, to_state)

## Templates

Print one with `rgen templates <pack>/<name> > file.toml`, edit, then `rgen run --config file.toml out.duckdb`.

- `logistics/air-cargo`: Air waybills between real airports: airline, flight, origin and destination, distance, pieces, actual and chargeable weight, rate and charges
- `logistics/ocean-containers`: Container bookings on real lanes: ISO 6346 container numbers, type, carrier, bill of lading, ports, distance, ETD/ETA/actual arrival, rate and Incoterm
- `logistics/parcels`: US parcel shipments: carrier, service, tracking number, origin and destination states, zone, weight, cost, transit, outcome, delivery time and JSON tracking history
- `logistics/warehouse-moves`: Warehouse movement log: SSCC labels, SKUs, bin locations, movement types and quantities by operator and shift
