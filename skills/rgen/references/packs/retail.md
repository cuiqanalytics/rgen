# Data pack: retail (Retail 1.0.0)

Generated by rgen-cli/site-packs.sh from the pack; edit lookup_data/packs/retail/skill.md in cuiqGEN instead.
Read only when `rgen packs list` shows this pack as licensed.

Use for retail and e-commerce data: product catalogs, orders and order lines, returns and store
inventory. An item number fixes everything about a product (category, brand, name, color, size,
price, cost, GTIN), so catalog, orders and inventory agree without joins. GTIN-13, UPC-A, GTIN-14
and ISBN-13 carry valid check digits and one GS1 company prefix per brand. Orders follow the retail
calendar (December, weekends, Black Friday), categories follow the season (swimwear in June, coats
and toys in December), and returns follow the category and channel. Brand names are invented; all
values are synthetic and illustrative.

## Chains (put each driver in its own column, then pass the column)

| `retail_channel` | `retail_fulfillment(channel)`, `retail_payment_method(channel)` |
| `retail_order_date(year)` + channel | `retail_order_time(order_date, channel)`, `retail_order_number(channel, order_date)` |
| order_time + fulfillment | `retail_delivery_date(order_time, fulfillment)` → `retail_order_status(order_time, delivery_date)` |
| item + order_date | `retail_discount_pct(item, order_date)` → `retail_unit_price(item, discount_pct)` |
| item + channel | `retail_returned(item, channel)` → `retail_return_reason(item, returned)` → `retail_return_condition(item, reason)`, `retail_refund(item, amount, reason)`; `retail_return_date(delivery_date, returned)` |
| store number | `retail_store_code(store)`, `retail_store_format(store)` |
| item + store_format | `retail_daily_demand(item, format)`, `retail_safety_stock(item, format)`, `retail_reorder_point(item, format)`, `retail_on_hand(item, format)` |

`retail_lead_time_days(item, 'dc')` for vendor lead times), `retail_shipping_cost(fulfillment, subtotal, loyalty_tier)`.

## Pitfalls

- Use the same catalog size everywhere: `retail_item_nth(seq, N)` with `rows = N` lists the
  catalog, and `retail_item(N, ...)` only sells items from it. Catalog sizes run from 63 (one
  item per category) to 30000.
- Join sales to the catalog on `item` (or `sku`/`gtin`); never re-draw attributes with
  `retail_item` inside another provider (`retail_category(retail_item())` re-draws per call).
- Pass `month(order_date)` to `retail_item` to get seasonal categories; without it the mix is
  the annual one.
- `retail_upc` is NULL for non-US brands and books (they have EANs and ISBNs); `retail_color`
  and `retail_size` are NULL where the category has none (groceries, books).
- `retail_return_reason`, `retail_return_date`, `retail_return_condition` and `retail_refund`
  are NULL for lines that were not returned.
- `retail_store_format(store)` is fixed per store number: keep the store number as a column and
  derive the code and format from it.

## All providers

Use in `-p` without the `rand_` prefix; in TOML `derived` expressions with it.

- `retail_bcountry(item)`: home country of the item's brand (ISO 3166 alpha-2, UK for the United Kingdom); sets the GS1 prefix — dependent: retail_brand_country(item)
- `retail_brand(item)`: brand of an item (invented names, 3-8 per department; publishers for books) — dependent: retail_brand(item)
- `retail_btier(item)`: price tier of the item's brand: value, mid or premium — dependent: retail_brand_tier(item)
- `retail_case_pack(item)`: units per shipping case (1 for furniture, TVs and other bulky goods) — dependent: retail_case_pack(item)
- `retail_category(item)`: category of an item (63 categories) — dependent: retail_category(item)
- `retail_channel()`: sales channel: store, web, app or marketplace
- `retail_color(item)`: color from the category's palette (shades for makeup, finishes for jewelry), NULL for groceries, books and other uncolored goods — dependent: retail_color(item)
- `retail_daily_demand(item,fmt)`: expected units sold per day of an item in a store format: high for produce and dairy, low for TVs and furniture, more for value brands and best sellers — dependent: retail_daily_demand(item, store_format)
- `retail_delivery_date(order_time,fulfil)`: date the customer receives the order: same day in store and for same-day delivery, same or next day for pickup, about 3 days shipped — dependent: retail_delivery_date(order_time, fulfillment)
- `retail_department(item)`: department of an item (Apparel, Electronics, Grocery, ...) — dependent: retail_department(item)
- `retail_discount_pct(item,order_date)`: discount percent on the line: promotions peak in November, December, January and July; out-of-season items clear at 30-70 percent; groceries stay shallow — dependent: retail_discount_pct(item, order_date)
- `retail_fulfillment(chan)`: fulfilment: in_store for store sales; ship_to_home, pickup_in_store, curbside or same_day_delivery online — dependent: retail_fulfillment(channel)
- `retail_gender(item)`: Men's, Women's or Kids' for apparel and footwear, NULL otherwise — dependent: retail_gender(item)
- `retail_gtin(item)`: GTIN-13 (EAN) with a valid check digit: GS1 prefix of the brand's country, one company prefix per brand, unique per item; ISBN-13 (978) for books — dependent: retail_gtin(item)
- `retail_gtin14(item,indicator)`: GTIN-14 of a case of the item (indicator digit 1-8, default 1) with a recomputed check digit — dependent: retail_gtin14(item, indicator)
- `retail_item(catalog,month)`: item sold from a catalog of the given size (default 2000): categories weighted by sales and, with month (1-12), by season; a few items per category sell most — dependent: retail_item(catalog, month)
- `retail_item_nth(n,catalog)`: n-th item (1 to catalog) of a catalog of the given size (default 2000, at least 63): number the rows with row_number() to list a whole catalog — dependent: retail_item_nth(n, catalog)
- `retail_lead_time_days(item,level)`: replenishment lead time in days: stores restock from the distribution centre in 2-7 days; level 'dc' gives the vendor lead time, 4-11 days for North American brands and 25-50 for imports; fresh food 1-2 — dependent: retail_lead_time_days(item, level)
- `retail_list_price(item)`: list price (USD) with charm endings (.99, .49, 129.99): typical of the category, higher for premium brands and bigger sizes — dependent: retail_list_price(item)
- `retail_loyalty_tier()`: loyalty tier: none, member, silver, gold or platinum
- `retail_on_hand(item,fmt)`: units on hand: about 3 days of demand for fresh food, 3-4 weeks otherwise; 4 percent of items out of stock — dependent: retail_on_hand(item, store_format)
- `retail_on_order(item,on_hand,reorder_point,fmt)`: units on open purchase orders: about 30 days of demand in whole cases when stock is at or below the reorder point, else 0 — dependent: retail_on_order(item, on_hand, reorder_point, store_format)
- `retail_order_date(yr)`: order date in a year (2022-2028, default 2025): more in December and on weekends, peaks on Black Friday, Cyber Monday and before Christmas, near zero on Dec 25 — dependent: retail_order_date(year)
- `retail_order_number(chan,order_date)`: order number with a channel letter and the order date (W250412-0048213) — dependent: retail_order_number(channel, order_date)
- `retail_order_status(order_time,delivery_date,as_of)`: order status on the as_of date (default 2026-09-30): processing, shipped until the delivery date, then completed; about 2.5 percent cancelled — dependent: retail_order_status(order_time, delivery_date, as_of)
- `retail_order_time(order_date,chan)`: order timestamp on the order date: store hours 8-21 with lunch and after-work peaks, online all day with an evening peak — dependent: retail_order_time(order_date, channel)
- `retail_payment_method(chan)`: payment method: cash and EBT only in store, buy now pay later only online — dependent: retail_payment_method(channel)
- `retail_product_name(item)`: product title: gender, brand, descriptor and type with color and size (Women's Northvale Merino Crew Sweater - Navy, M); title and format for books — dependent: retail_product_name(item)
- `retail_product_type(item)`: product type within the category (Air Fryer, Chelsea Boot; the genre for books) — dependent: retail_product_type(item)
- `retail_quantity(item)`: units on an order line: up to 6-8 for groceries, 1 for TVs and furniture — dependent: retail_quantity(item)
- `retail_refund(item,amount,reason)`: refund (USD): the amount paid, less a 15 percent restocking fee on electronics and furniture returned without a fault; NULL when not returned — dependent: retail_refund(item, amount, reason)
- `retail_reorder_point(item,fmt)`: reorder point in units: demand over the lead time plus safety stock — dependent: retail_reorder_point(item, store_format)
- `retail_return_condition(item,reason)`: disposition of a return: resellable, open_box, damaged, return_to_vendor (defects) or disposed (food, health and beauty); NULL when not returned — dependent: retail_return_condition(item, reason)
- `retail_return_date(delivery_date,returned)`: date the item came back: days after delivery, within the 45-day window (extended for holiday purchases); NULL when not returned — dependent: retail_return_date(delivery_date, returned)
- `retail_return_reason(item,returned)`: return reason typical of the category (too_small for apparel, defective for electronics, damaged in furniture, expired for groceries, wrong_shade for makeup); NULL when not returned — dependent: retail_return_reason(item, returned)
- `retail_returned(item,chan)`: whether the line is returned: about a quarter of online apparel and footwear, 10 percent of electronics, almost no groceries; store sales a third as often — dependent: retail_returned(item, channel)
- `retail_safety_stock(item,fmt)`: safety stock in units for a 95 percent service level over the lead time — dependent: retail_safety_stock(item, store_format)
- `retail_shipping_cost(fulfil,subtotal,tier)`: shipping charge (USD): free over 35 and for gold or platinum members, 5.99 otherwise, 9.99 same-day, 0 for pickup and in store — dependent: retail_shipping_cost(fulfillment, subtotal, loyalty_tier)
- `retail_size(item)`: size in the category's system: XS-XXL, waist x inseam, US shoe sizes by gender, kids 2T-14, or capacity, screen, bed, volume and count sizes; NULL when the category has none — dependent: retail_size(item)
- `retail_sku(item)`: internal SKU: category code, item, color and size codes (SWT-0042-NAV-M) — dependent: retail_sku(item)
- `retail_stock_status(on_hand,reorder_point)`: stock status: out_of_stock, low_stock (at or below the reorder point), in_stock or overstock — dependent: retail_stock_status(on_hand, reorder_point)
- `retail_store_code(store)`: store code ST0001 for a store number — dependent: retail_store_code(store)
- `retail_store_format(store)`: store format for a store number (same number, same format): superstore, standard, express or outlet — dependent: retail_store_format(store)
- `retail_unit_cost(item)`: unit cost (USD): the category's cost ratio of list price (thin margins on groceries and TVs, wide on beauty and jewelry) — dependent: retail_unit_cost(item)
- `retail_unit_price(item,discount_pct)`: selling price (USD): list price less the discount — dependent: retail_unit_price(item, discount_pct)
- `retail_upc(item)`: 12-digit UPC-A for items of US brands (the GTIN-13 without its leading 0), NULL otherwise — dependent: retail_upc(item)
- `retail_weight_kg(item)`: shipping weight in kg, typical of the category and size — dependent: retail_weight_kg(item)
