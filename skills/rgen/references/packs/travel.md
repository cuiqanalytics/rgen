# Data pack: travel (Travel 1.0.0)

Generated by rgen-cli/site-packs.sh from the pack; edit lookup_data/packs/travel/skill.md in cuiqGEN instead.
Read only when `rgen packs list` shows this pack as licensed.

Use for travel data: airline schedules, bookings with PNRs, tickets and fares, flight operations
(delays, cancellations, load factors) and hotel stays. A flight number (1-9999) fixes the scheduled
flight: a real airline (23, with real IATA codes and ticketing prefixes), one end at one of its
hubs, the other a domestic airport at least 250 km away or an international gateway, great-circle
distance, aircraft for the stage length, departure time and block time. Fares follow distance,
cabin, lead time, season and weekday; business travelers book later and fly premium more;
refundable fares are cancelled more. Hotel rates follow class, country and season. Airports come
from OurAirports. Passengers, bookings and stays are synthetic; hotel chain names are invented.

## Pitfalls

- Flight numbers run from 1 to 9999; use the same range for schedules and bookings.
- For a flight's cities use `travel_origin_city(flight)`, not `travel_airport_city(origin)` on a
  derived column (rgen inlines the derived expression; the flight-keyed providers are much faster).
- Fares are USD before taxes; `total = fare + taxes`.
- Departure times are local and schedules repeat daily; time zones are not modelled.

## All providers

Use in `-p` without the `rand_` prefix; in TOML `derived` expressions with it.

- `travel_aircraft(f)`: aircraft type for the stage length: regional jets and A320/737 short-haul, 787/A330 medium long-haul, 777/A350/A380 ultra long-haul — dependent: travel_aircraft(flight)
- `travel_airline(f)`: airline name — dependent: travel_airline(flight)
- `travel_airline_code(f)`: IATA airline designator of the flight (AA, LH, EK, ...), weighted by airline size — dependent: travel_airline_code(flight)
- `travel_airline_model(f)`: network or low_cost carrier — dependent: travel_airline_model(flight)
- `travel_airport_city(code)`: city served by an airport — dependent: travel_airport_city(iata)
- `travel_airport_country(code)`: country of an airport (ISO 3166 alpha-2) — dependent: travel_airport_country(iata)
- `travel_airport_name(code)`: airport name for an IATA code — dependent: travel_airport_name(iata)
- `travel_block_minutes(f)`: scheduled gate-to-gate minutes from the distance (cruise about 810 km/h plus taxi), rounded to 5 — dependent: travel_block_minutes(flight)
- `travel_booking_channel(purp)`: where the ticket was sold: corporate travel agencies for business, airline site and app or online travel agencies for leisure — dependent: travel_booking_channel(purpose)
- `travel_booking_status(fare_class,dep_date,as_of)`: booking status: flown, no_show, cancelled (more often on refundable fares), or ticketed/changed for future departures — dependent: travel_booking_status(fare_class, departure_date, as_of)
- `travel_cabin(f,purp)`: cabin: premium cabins mostly for business trips, premium economy and first only on long-haul, economy only on low-cost carriers — dependent: travel_cabin(flight, purpose)
- `travel_delay_cause(delay_minutes,status)`: delay or cancellation cause (US BTS categories: late_aircraft, carrier, nas, weather, security) for delays of 15 minutes or more; NULL otherwise — dependent: travel_delay_cause(delay_minutes, status)
- `travel_delay_minutes(f,dep_date,status)`: departure delay in minutes (negative = early); delays grow through the day and in summer and December, with a long tail; NULL if not operated — dependent: travel_delay_minutes(flight, departure_date, status)
- `travel_departure_time(f)`: scheduled local departure time: morning and evening banks, late-night departures for some long-haul flights — dependent: travel_departure_time(flight)
- `travel_destination(f)`: destination airport (IATA): mostly domestic (at least 250 km) for US carriers, international gateways for Gulf and Asian carriers — dependent: travel_destination(flight)
- `travel_destination_city(f)`: city of the flight's destination airport — dependent: travel_destination_city(flight)
- `travel_distance_km(f)`: great-circle distance of the flight in km — dependent: travel_distance_km(flight)
- `travel_fare(f,cabin,lead_days,dep_date)`: base fare in USD: rises with distance and cabin, higher for last-minute bookings, summer and December, Fridays and Sundays; about 30 percent lower on low-cost carriers — dependent: travel_fare(flight, cabin, lead_days, departure_date)
- `travel_fare_class(cabin,lead_days)`: booking class: full fares (Y, B, J, F) close in, discounted buckets (Q, V, K, L, T, I) booked early — dependent: travel_fare_class(cabin, lead_days)
- `travel_flight(n)`: random flight number from 1 to n (default 2000, at most 9999); every flight provider derives the scheduled flight from it
- `travel_flight_number(f)`: marketed flight number (UA1432), unique per flight number up to 9999 — dependent: travel_flight_number(flight)
- `travel_flight_status(f,dep_date)`: operated, cancelled (about 1.5 percent, twice as often in winter) or diverted — dependent: travel_flight_status(flight, departure_date)
- `travel_gate(code)`: departure gate (B23); more concourses at hubs — dependent: travel_gate(airport)
- `travel_hotel_channel()`: hotel booking channel: direct, ota, gds or wholesale
- `travel_hotel_city()`: destination of a stay as the IATA code of a large airport, weighted toward hub cities (travel_airport_city gives the city name)
- `travel_hotel_name(tier,code)`: hotel name: an invented chain for the class plus the city (Harbor Suites Chicago) — dependent: travel_hotel_name(tier, iata)
- `travel_hotel_tier()`: hotel class: economy, midscale, upscale or luxury
- `travel_international(f)`: whether the flight crosses a border — dependent: travel_international(flight)
- `travel_lead_days(purp)`: days between booking and departure: about 12 for business trips, 5-6 weeks for leisure — dependent: travel_lead_days(purpose)
- `travel_load_factor(f,dep_date)`: share of seats filled (about 83 percent; fuller in summer, December, Fridays and Sundays) — dependent: travel_load_factor(flight, departure_date)
- `travel_loyalty_tier()`: frequent-flyer tier: none, basic, silver, gold or platinum
- `travel_miles(f,fare_class,tier)`: frequent-flyer miles earned: flown miles times the fare-class and tier bonuses, 0 for non-members — dependent: travel_miles(flight, fare_class, tier)
- `travel_nightly_rate(tier,code,checkin)`: average nightly rate in USD for the class, the country's price level and the season — dependent: travel_nightly_rate(tier, iata, checkin)
- `travel_nights(purp)`: length of stay in nights: about 2 for business, 4 for leisure — dependent: travel_nights(purpose)
- `travel_origin(f)`: origin airport (IATA): one end of every flight is a hub of the airline — dependent: travel_origin(flight)
- `travel_origin_city(f)`: city of the flight's origin airport — dependent: travel_origin_city(flight)
- `travel_pnr()`: 6-character booking reference (record locator) without look-alike characters (no 0, 1, I, O)
- `travel_purpose()`: trip purpose: leisure, business or visiting_friends_relatives
- `travel_rate_plan()`: hotel rate plan: flexible, non_refundable, corporate or package
- `travel_refund(fare_class,total,status)`: amount refunded: all of a refundable fare (Y, B, W, J, C, F), taxes only (about 15 percent) for discounted fares, 0 unless cancelled — dependent: travel_refund(fare_class, total, status)
- `travel_room_type(tier)`: room type (more suites at luxury hotels) — dependent: travel_room_type(tier)
- `travel_seat(f,cabin)`: seat for the cabin and aircraft: rows 1-2 first, business up front, letters A-K on wide-bodies, A-F on narrow-bodies, A-D on regional jets — dependent: travel_seat(flight, cabin)
- `travel_seats(f)`: seats on the aircraft type — dependent: travel_seats(flight)
- `travel_stay_status(rate_plan,channel)`: stayed, cancelled (a third of flexible OTA bookings, few non-refundable) or no_show — dependent: travel_stay_status(rate_plan, channel)
- `travel_taxes(f,fare)`: taxes, fees and charges in USD (higher on international flights) — dependent: travel_taxes(flight, fare)
- `travel_ticket_number(f)`: 13-digit e-ticket number starting with the airline's 3-digit ticketing prefix (001 American, 220 Lufthansa, 176 Emirates) — dependent: travel_ticket_number(flight)
