# Data pack: weather (Weather & Climate 1.0.0)

Generated by rgen-cli/site-packs.sh from the pack; edit lookup_data/packs/weather/skill.md in cuiqGEN instead.
Read only when `rgen packs list` shows this pack as licensed.

Use for weather and climate data: station catalogs, daily or hourly observations, IoT sensor
feeds, and weather columns for demand, energy, logistics or retail models. There are 105 stations
in real cities across five climate zones (tropical, arid, temperate, continental, polar).
Coordinates are approximate and climate normals are rounded, illustrative values. Station ids and
names are invented. None of the values are real observations.

## Chains (put each driver in its own column, then pass the column)

| Driver column | Dependent providers |
|---|---|
| station (`weather_station`, `weather_station_in(zone)`, `weather_station_nth(i)`) | `weather_station_name/city/country/country_code/lat/lon/elevation/zone/hemisphere(station)` |
| station + date | `weather_temp_min`, `weather_temp_max`, `weather_temp_mean`, `weather_daily_sky`, `weather_daylight_hours` |
| station + timestamp | `weather_temperature_at`, `weather_sky_at` (reads 'clear' at night), `weather_is_daylight` |
| sky (any of the above, or `weather_sky(zone, month)`) | `weather_precipitation_mm(sky[, hours[, zone]])`, `weather_humidity(sky, zone)`, `weather_wind_kmh(sky)`, `weather_pressure_hpa(sky)`, `weather_visibility_km(sky)` |
| precipitation / wind / temperature + humidity | `weather_snowfall_cm(sky, precip)`, `weather_wind_gust_kmh(wind)`, `weather_dew_point(temp, rh)`, `weather_feels_like(temp, rh, wind)` |

Without stations: `weather_climate_zone()`, `weather_season(month[, 'S'])`, `weather_sky(zone, month[, 'S'])`,
`weather_temperature(zone, month[, 'S'])`. Plus `weather_wind_direction()` (degrees) and `weather_compass(deg)`.

The station providers are deterministic: the same station and date give the same weather in every
column, table and run. Temperatures persist over several days, follow the station's seasonal curve
(flipped in the southern hemisphere) and change smoothly from hour to hour. Snow only falls when it
is cold enough, and never in tropical or arid zones.

Templates: `weather/stations`, `/daily` (12 stations x 365 days), `/hourly` (10 stations x 90 days
x 24 h), `/sensor-feed` (random stations and times, with dropouts and error codes).

## Pitfalls

- To build a station x time grid, index rows with `row_number() OVER ()` and use
  `weather_station_nth(i % N)` with `rows` = N x periods, as the daily and hourly templates do. Keep
  N, the `% N` / `// N` and `rows` in sync. The first N stations cover the zones evenly.
- Pass a column, not a nested random call: `weather_humidity(weather_sky(...), ...)` draws a
  different sky than the one in your sky column.
- `weather_sky` and `weather_temperature` are independent draws for a zone and month and do not
  agree with each other. When sky, temperature and snow have to be consistent, use the station
  providers.
- For daily totals call `weather_precipitation_mm(sky, 24, zone)`. Passing the zone keeps annual
  totals realistic (light rain in deserts, tropical downpours). Without it every zone rains alike.
- Months are integers 1-12 (`month(ts)`). Pass `'S'` for southern-hemisphere seasons when you use
  the zone-level providers. The station providers already know their hemisphere.
- Timestamps are treated as local solar time: the daily low is around 05:00, the high around 15:00,
  and daylight is centred on 12:00.

## All providers

Use in `-p` without the `rand_` prefix; in TOML `derived` expressions with it.

- `weather_climate_zone()`: climate zone: tropical, arid, temperate, continental or polar, roughly by populated land share
- `weather_compass(deg)`: 16-point compass label (N, NNE, ... NNW) of a wind direction in degrees — dependent: weather_compass(wind_direction)
- `weather_daily_sky(st,d)`: prevailing sky of a station on a date, consistent with its temperatures (snow only below freezing-ish) — dependent: weather_daily_sky(station_id, date)
- `weather_daylight_hours(st,d)`: hours of daylight at a station on a date (0 in polar night, 24 in midnight sun) — dependent: weather_daylight_hours(station_id, date)
- `weather_dew_point(t_c,rh)`: dew point in C from temperature and relative humidity (Magnus formula), never above the temperature — dependent: weather_dew_point(temperature_c, humidity)
- `weather_feels_like(t_c,rh,wind)`: apparent ("feels like") temperature in C from temperature, humidity and wind in km/h (Steadman apparent temperature) — dependent: weather_feels_like(temperature_c, humidity, wind_kmh)
- `weather_humidity(sk,z)`: relative humidity in % (5-100) for a sky condition and climate zone (dry deserts, saturated fog) — dependent: weather_humidity(sky, zone)
- `weather_is_daylight(st,ts)`: true when the sun is up at a station and timestamp (local solar time) — dependent: weather_is_daylight(station_id, ts)
- `weather_precipitation_mm(sk,hours,climate)`: precipitation in mm (water equivalent) for a sky condition over hours := 1 (24 for daily totals; storms are short so they scale less), optionally scaled by climate zone (tropical downpours, light desert rain); 0 when not precipitating — dependent: weather_precipitation_mm(sky[, hours[, zone]])
- `weather_pressure_hpa(sk)`: sea-level air pressure in hPa for a sky condition (high under clear skies, low in storms) — dependent: weather_pressure_hpa(sky)
- `weather_season(mon,hemi)`: meteorological season (winter, spring, summer, autumn) of a month 1-12, flipped for hemi := 'S' — dependent: weather_season(month)
- `weather_sky(z,mon,hemi)`: sky condition (sunny, partly cloudy, cloudy, fog, drizzle, rain, storm, snow, sleet, dust) weighted by climate zone and season; no snow in tropical or arid zones — dependent: weather_sky(zone, month[, hemi])
- `weather_sky_at(st,ts)`: sky at a station and timestamp: follows the day's prevailing sky most of the time, changes in 6-hour blocks, 'clear' instead of sunny at night — dependent: weather_sky_at(station_id, ts)
- `weather_snowfall_cm(sk,precip)`: fresh snow in cm from a sky condition and its precipitation (about 1 cm per mm for snow), 0 otherwise — dependent: weather_snowfall_cm(sky, precipitation_mm)
- `weather_station()`: weather station id (e.g. GB-LON-42) in one of 105 cities across all climate zones
- `weather_station_city(st)`: city of a station — dependent: weather_station_city(station_id)
- `weather_station_country(st)`: country (or territory) of a station — dependent: weather_station_country(station_id)
- `weather_station_country_code(st)`: ISO 3166-1 alpha-2 code of a station's country — dependent: weather_station_country_code(station_id)
- `weather_station_elevation(st)`: station elevation in metres — dependent: weather_station_elevation(station_id)
- `weather_station_hemisphere(st)`: hemisphere of a station, N or S (seasons are flipped in S) — dependent: weather_station_hemisphere(station_id)
- `weather_station_in(z)`: weather station id in a climate zone: tropical, arid, temperate, continental or polar — dependent: weather_station_in(zone)
- `weather_station_lat(st)`: station latitude in decimal degrees — dependent: weather_station_lat(station_id)
- `weather_station_lon(st)`: station longitude in decimal degrees — dependent: weather_station_lon(station_id)
- `weather_station_name(st)`: station name such as "Madrid Observatory" — dependent: weather_station_name(station_id)
- `weather_station_nth(i)`: the i-th station (0-based, wraps around); the order interleaves zones so any first 5k stations cover all five evenly — use for station x time grids: weather_station_nth(i)
- `weather_station_zone(st)`: climate zone of a station (tropical, arid, temperate, continental, polar) — dependent: weather_station_zone(station_id)
- `weather_temp_max(st,d)`: daily maximum temperature in C of a station on a date (smaller swing on cloudy days) — dependent: weather_temp_max(station_id, date)
- `weather_temp_mean(st,d)`: daily mean temperature in C of a station on a date (seasonal normal + multi-day anomaly + sky effect) — dependent: weather_temp_mean(station_id, date)
- `weather_temp_min(st,d)`: daily minimum temperature in C of a station on a date — dependent: weather_temp_min(station_id, date)
- `weather_temperature(z,mon,hemi)`: air temperature in C at a random time of day for a climate zone and month 1-12 (zone normal + day anomaly + diurnal swing); hemi := 'S' flips seasons — dependent: weather_temperature(zone, month[, hemi])
- `weather_temperature_at(st,ts)`: air temperature in C at a station and timestamp: seasonal, multi-day and diurnal (min ~05:00, max ~15:00) cycles, smooth hour to hour — dependent: weather_temperature_at(station_id, ts)
- `weather_visibility_km(sk)`: horizontal visibility in km (max 50) for a sky condition: under 1 km in fog, low in snow and dust — dependent: weather_visibility_km(sky)
- `weather_wind_direction()`: wind direction in degrees (0-350 in 10 degree steps, 0 = from the north), prevailing westerly/south-westerly
- `weather_wind_gust_kmh(wind)`: peak gust in km/h, always above the mean wind — dependent: weather_wind_gust_kmh(wind_kmh)
- `weather_wind_kmh(sk)`: mean wind speed in km/h for a sky condition (calm fog, windy storms and dust) — dependent: weather_wind_kmh(sky)

## Templates

Print one with `rgen templates <pack>/<name> > file.toml`, edit, then `rgen run --config file.toml out.duckdb`.

- `weather/daily`: One row per station and day for a year: min/max/mean temperature, prevailing sky, precipitation, snowfall, humidity, wind, pressure, daylight
- `weather/hourly`: Hourly readings per station for a quarter: temperature, sky (clear at night), precipitation, humidity, dew point, wind, pressure, visibility, feels-like
- `weather/sensor-feed`: IoT-style feed: random stations and irregular timestamps over a year, with sensor faults (dropouts, error codes) and corrupted battery values
- `weather/stations`: Station catalog: id, name, city, country, coordinates, elevation, climate zone, hemisphere
